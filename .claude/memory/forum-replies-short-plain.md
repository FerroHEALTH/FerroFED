On FerroTERM on 2026-09-12 a 408-word forum draft was rejected hard, and a
186-word one the same day as "so much like AI". A reply drafted for the owner
stays near 100 to 150 words and reads like a person typing.

**Why:** the owner posts under their own name on the openEHR Discourse and is
judged on it. Feedback on the Federation Tier specification is likely to go
there or to the specification's tracker, in the owner's name.

**How to apply:** running prose with contractions, one thought per paragraph,
no bulleted points, no neat parallel sentences, no closing question that
surveys the options; a single plain question at the end if one is needed.
Address people by their @handle. Every specification the reply leans on gets
an inline link to the exact section anchor, checked live before posting.
This is a draft for the owner to post; it does not override
[[upstream-reports-stay-here]], which forbids filing on external trackers from
the session.
