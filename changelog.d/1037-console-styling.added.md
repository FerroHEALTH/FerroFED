- The operator console is styled on the "Azure & Iron" brand tokens, in a
  light and a dark theme that follow the system setting: a top bar with the
  brand mark and Sign out, the navigation beside the page (a wrapping band
  on a phone), tables with a header row and row separators, labelled form
  fields, refusals and incomplete answers in boxes that say what they are,
  and health states as words in a tinted cell. Every page fits a 390 px
  screen: a wide table scrolls inside the page, never the page itself. The
  stylesheet is Tailwind CSS 4.3.3, built by cargo-leptos with no Node and
  no new JavaScript (#1037).
