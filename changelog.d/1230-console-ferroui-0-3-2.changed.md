- The operator console is rebuilt on FerroUI 0.3.2 (`ferrohealth-ui`, pinned exactly) in islands mode (#1230, FerroHEALTH/FerroUI#161). The server draws every page whole from FerroUI's components, stylesheet and FerroFED theme, and the browser hydrates only FerroUI's interactive parts and the AQL editor, so every page reads and every action works with no WebAssembly. Every action is a plain form post to the page it sits on, answered by that page drawn again with the outcome at its top; a form the console or a replica refuses is drawn again as you typed it, the refused field marked, and Add and Remove of a row post the form. The pages follow FerroUI's reviewed FerroFED design: a dashboard, the topology, the organisations with their nodes (each node with its endpoints and its routes), the services, the integrity incidents, the stored queries with their versions, the query console, the self-description on five tabs with the manufacturer the gateway declares, the configuration, Your preferences and a find in the top bar. The top bar names the replica that answered. The site bundle is built with `cargo leptos build --split`.
