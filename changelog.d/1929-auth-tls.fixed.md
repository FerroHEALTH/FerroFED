- `[auth.tls]` is read: `trust_roots_file`, `client_identity_file` and `min_version` go through the family's TLS builder and apply to every issuer's metadata and key set, and an introspection grant's own `tls` table to its calls, so an issuer behind a private certificate authority is trusted without `SSL_CERT_FILE`. 0.0.20 refused the table, and its book documented `auth.ca_file`, a key no release since 0.0.19 reads (#1929).
