- The FHIR face serves a prescription only when it conforms to the MyHealth@EU ePrescription profile `MedicationRequest-myhealtheu-epd` and carries its narrative and language, the copy of the original (MyHealth@EU Requirements Catalogue v10.0.0 §1.14.1 and §1.14.2). A member's prescription that fails is left out of the search, with a `warning` `outcome` entry naming the member and what is missing, and its read answers `424`. In the map mode the gateway writes the FHIR R4 `data-absent-reason` `unknown` on `identifier`, `authoredOn` or `requester` when the order gives none, the composition's language, and a narrative of the order's own texts. Rows 06.01 and 06.02 of the MyHealth@EU self-assessment are met.
