- The operator console draws with `ferrohealth-ui` 0.1.4. Every date-time
  it shows (an incident, a cleared collision, a revision, a confirmation
  deadline, a signature and a date-time in a query's rows) reads on one
  line as the library draws it, moved to UTC and cut to the second, with
  the value as the gateway sent it in its title. The query result rows
  stack as cards on a phone like the other tables, each cell named by its
  column, and keep their pages. On a phone the dashboard's tiles are
  compact one-line tiles, and a disabled field keeps its edge in the dark
  theme.
