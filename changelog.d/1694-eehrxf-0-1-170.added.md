- The FHIR face receives medical imaging reports, Art 14(1)(d), and
  discharge reports, Art 14(1)(f), as documents at `POST {fhir-base}/Bundle`:
  each is held to its HL7 Europe document profiles (Imaging Report
  1.0.0-ballot, Hospital Discharge Report 1.0.0), mapped by the projects
  `eehrxf` 0.1.170 ships and written to the member a `[[fhir.receive]]`
  declares for its category. The information sheet lists both as received.
