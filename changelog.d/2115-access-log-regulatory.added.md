- `[access_log.regulatory]` declares the legal basis the gateway processes under (the GDPR Art 6(1) point and Art 9(2) condition) and its controllers and processors, and every access record states them on its categories entity (`ehds-legal-basis`, `ehds-legal-condition`, `ehds-controller`, `ehds-processor`; Xt-EHR D5.2 §7.2.7). Nothing is declared by default, and a record then writes none.
