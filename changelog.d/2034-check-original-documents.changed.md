- The FHIR face checks a member's own laboratory, discharge and imaging documents before it serves them (MyHealth@EU Requirements Catalogue v10.0.0 §1.16 and §1.16.1). A `DocumentReference` that lacks the header data of the MyHealth@EU `DocumentReference-minimal-myhealtheu` profile, or an attachment media type, is not listed, and an `outcome` entry names the member in `node-error`. A `Binary/{token}` read serves an original only as a PDF/A-1 (draft exchange-format implementing act Annex II), a JPEG or PNG image, or for imaging a DICOM file, whose bytes match the media type the member names; any other answer is refused with `424`, naming the member. Rows 08 and 08.01 of the MyHealth@EU self-assessment are met.
